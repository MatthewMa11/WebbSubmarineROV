An educational view, not a vehicle depth rating. Light-zone boundaries follow NOAA’s ocean light zones. Pressure uses a rounded estimate of 1 atmosphere at the surface plus about 1 per 10 m, from NOAA’s pressure lesson. The ROV view uses the club’s CAD assembly.
