R12 - ONE-PIECE FRAME AND OUTER DUCT

The former gray frame and blue 28mm outer duct are now one connected printed
housing. A continuous internal seam web replaces their mating clearance; it
adds about2,512mm3 of material outside the rotating envelope. The housing has
one CAD body and one closed connected print mesh. Installed outside dimensions

The fan stays separate so it can spin; the gold cap remains removable for access.
Actual motor, collet, collar, bullet nut and all40 fastener bodies retain their

Assembly, power disconnected
1. Prepare the matching actual metal motor/adapter and R11 fan on the bench.
   Put the collar's broad flange toward the fan, with its narrow tapered sleeve
   toward the motor. Fit the fan's6.2mm shallow rear recess toward the collar.
   Its5.1mm through-bore slides over the source adapter's roughly4.83mm stud.
   Install the bullet nut flat base against the hub and nose pointing forward.
   Follow the adapter maker's grip/engagement instructions; hold the metal bell
   rather than the blades while tightening. Check slip/pull-off by hand.
2. With the gold cap off, insert the motor/fan through the new housing's front
   opening. The former extension is already integral, so no seam alignment or
   separate extension installation is needed. Route the real flexible wires
   through the unchanged rear openings, away from all rotating parts.
3. Secure the motor base with all four M3x8 bolts and two washers under EACH
   head. Tighten evenly. R11's2mm nominal engagement and0.3876mm modeled blind-
   bottom margin remain unchanged; confirm real screws/holes before tightening.
4. Put the captured cap nuts and rear washers in their existing seats before
   closing. Position the unchanged cap and insert the four M3x40 bolts with
   front washers through the cap and one-piece housing. Tighten in a cross
   pattern. The original fastener paths and clearance pockets remain open.
5. Turn several complete revolutions by hand. Inspect every tip, motor bell,
   collet/nut, wire and surface for rubbing and axial play. The fan must not
   slip independently on the adapter. Recheck after a restrained low-speed run.
