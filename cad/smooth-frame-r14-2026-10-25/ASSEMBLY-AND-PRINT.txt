Assembly - same working R13 joints
1. Keep power disconnected. Install the actual motor/fan/metal adapter through
   the front opening before fitting the cone. Use the existing four M3x8 motor-
   base bolts with TWO0.5mm flat washers beneath each head. External mounting
   hardware remains unchanged. Keep wires out of rotating parts.
2. At each cone joint, slide an M3 nut plus its rear flat washer into the housing
   post from the OUTSIDE. Washer:7mm OD x3.2mm ID x0.5mm thick. Use a flat washer,
   not a split spring washer. Nut flats must be captured by the narrower slot.
3. Seat the cone sockets over the four pilots; the flat feet must bear on the
   housing seats. Nominal radial and end pilot relief is0.2mm. Do not force it.
4. Put one front flat washer under each M3x12 screw head. Start all four screws
   into their captured nuts, then tighten gradually in a cross pattern, only
   enough to seat the parts firmly. Do not crush the PLA. Do not use M3x40 here.
5. The four old deep nut locations and C-shaped lugs are gone. Use only the front
   nut seats described above. Expected full nut engagement is2.4mm and screw
   tip projection0.9mm. Verify your real hardware with the small joint prints.
6. Rotate the fan through several full turns by hand and inspect all clearances
   before a restrained low-speed test. Recheck retention after the first run.
