What changed
The fan has a flat 6.5 mm hub with a 6.2 mm clearance bore, clamped between the
metal backplate and washer. There are no printed shaft-clamp jaws, motor vent
keys, collar, jam ring or internal motor spacer. The unchanged cap moves
28 mm forward on a separate duct extension. The frame stays unchanged in
shape and position. Existing prints of these same R6.1 frame / R4 cap revisions
can be reused; older physical revisions may have different cover joints.

Check the bought adapter BEFORE printing the full fan
1. Confirm that the actual motor shaft is the 3.17 mm / 1/8 inch size.
2. Measure the adapter's usable input-bore depth; this is not published on
   the product page. Check that the collet fully engages the actual shaft.
   Do not substitute partial engagement just to make the cap fit.
3. The CAD assumes the metal backplate's fan seating face is 10.3 mm forward
   of the motor's front bell face, nominally Y24.3 versus bell face Y14.
   The clearance study accepts this distance from 10.3 through 13.3 mm
   (Y24.3..27.3) with the WHOLE adapter/fan stack shifted together.
   Measure the installed seating face with the collet fully engaged.
   Outside that range, stop and revise the CAD; do not force the parts.
4. Print TEST-FIRST-R10-6p2-bore.stl and check it slips onto the M6 prop stud
   and seats flat against the metal backplate. The same 6.5 mm thickness and
   6.2 mm bore are used in the fan. A printed hole can shrink or be rough.

Assembly order (power disconnected, cap removed)
1. On the bench, place the correct metal collet adapter on the motor shaft.
   Keep it clear of stationary motor parts and follow the adapter maker's
   engagement/tightening instructions. Verify the measured seating-face range.
2. Slide the fan's flat rear face against the metal backplate. The 6.2 mm
   bore is a clearance hole, not a printed thread. Put the supplied metal
   washer on the other hub face, then install the supplied M6 nut.
3. Hold the metal motor bell, not the blades, while tightening the nut.
   Nut pressure clamps the hub and draws the collet onto the shaft.
   There is no verified tightening torque for this PLA hub: do not crush it.
   Confirm the fan cannot slip or pull off by hand. The washer must bear
   flat on the hub; the nut must fully engage the metal thread.
4. Fit the motor/fan assembly into the unchanged frame from the front,
   route the motor wires through the existing rear openings, and secure
   the motor base using its four existing M3 screws and washers. Check
   the rotating bell is clear and the wires stay out of the blade sweep.
5. Install the four captive M3 nuts and rear washers into the frame first.
   Slide the new 28 mm duct extension over the fan. Align its four holes
   with the frame's existing four axial cover holes. Position the unchanged
   cap on the extension, then insert the four M3x40 bolts through the
   front washers, cap, extension and frame into the existing rear
   washers/captive M3 nuts. Tighten evenly in a cross pattern.
   New locating pockets include print relief checked at 0.2 mm side offsets;
   remove support residue and dry-fit before tightening. Do not force a joint.
6. Turn the fan slowly by hand through several full revolutions, checking
   every blade tip, the motor bell, hub, adapter stud and all wires. Check
   axial play as well. There must be no rubbing at any angle.
7. Recheck the nut and hub retention after the first brief controlled run.
   Qualification must include the intended rotation/reversal direction.
   CAD clearance does not verify collet grip, rotor balance or PLA creep.

Bambu P1S / PLA Basic starting settings, subject to slicer inspection
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers, 5 walls, 6 top/bottom layers. Use solid infill
in the hub and extension bolt bosses. Inspect blade undersides and enable
build-plate supports where needed; do not put supports inside the prop bore.
Use no whole-model scaling or hole compensation that changes blade size.
Remove all support residue from the blade tips and inner duct surfaces.

Verification scope
Native assembly: 45 modeled solids, stationary group40 and rotating group5;
full revolution and no nominal interferences. Independent envelope includes
0.07 mm allowance plus the complete 0..3 mm forward installation range.
The metal adapter is a published-dimension clearance reference, not exact
manufacturer collet-taper CAD. Actual fit/engagement must pass the checks above.
No guarantee of clamp force, dynamic balance, thrust, PLA service life or
water sealing follows from the CAD model or mesh checks.
