R10 metal-collet fan revision
Approved: use a documented normal metal collet adapter; retain frame/cap shapes;
move unchanged cap forward and add a duct extension; no internal motor spacer.

Selected bought part: Innov8tive Designs CPA-317-N, 3.17 mm input / M6x1 stud,
25 mm overall, 17 mm usable stud, 15.5 mm backplate and washer, max hub 10.5 mm.
Source: https://innov8tivedesigns.com/collet-style-prop-adapter-with-hex-nut-for-3-17mm-motor-shafts.html

Proposed CAD: 6.2 mm clearance bore in a flat 6.5 mm fan hub; no printed jaws,
drive keys, internal spacer, collar or jam ring. Adapter backplate at Y24.3,
fan Y24.3..30.8, stud ends Y41.3. Outer blade profiles translate from R9 by
11.5 mm. Preserve blade radial size and root blend. Both clamping faces flat.

Cap moves +28 mm with shape unchanged, giving nominal 6.7 mm axial clearance
from adapter stud to cap rear plane. New 28 mm duct extension, four M3x40
through-bolts with existing washers/captive nuts. Frame unchanged in place.
New extension locating pockets are enlarged with a nine-copy 0.4 mm X/Z grid
spanning +/-0.4 mm, above the unchanged Y19 bearing plane. Verify zero frame
penetration at 0.2 mm lateral offsets in 24 directions (15-degree intervals).
An explicit R40 rear mouth to Y23.5, tapering to R38.8 at Y26.5, clears the
thin inherited R39 lip features that discrete-copy relief alone could miss.
All four R5 bolt-bearing columns are preserved; lead-in edges rounded0.2mm.
The M3x40 screws are additional purchased hardware, not assumed in the user's
existing 6..20 mm assortment. Adapter hardware is metal, not printed.

CAD adapter is a clearance/reference model, not a manufacturer machining model:
backplate/body bounded by published OD and 8 mm derived rear length; collet
taper and actual shaft insertion depth are not published. The complete rotating
envelope accommodates 0..3 mm forward movement of the whole adapter/fan stack,
leaving 3.63 mm minimum axial cap clearance including 0.07 mm allowance.
A physical fit check must measure actual usable bore depth and installed metal
backplate plane: accept Y24.3..27.3 only with the collet fully engaged on the
actual shaft. Short/partial grip is not accepted just because the cap clears.
This must be checked on the purchased adapter before printing/final assembly.
No asserted guarantee of clamp force, PLA fatigue life, thrust or watertightness.

Checks: unchanged frame; translated-identical cap; one-piece valid fan/extension;
adapter stack/bore/hub thickness; no old clamp or internal spacer; zero assembly
intersections; conservative full-turn envelope; fastener engagement and access;
native assembly groups/revolute; manifold millimetre STLs; export/package match.
