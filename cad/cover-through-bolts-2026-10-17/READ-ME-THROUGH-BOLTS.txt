A2212 thruster R4 - front cover through-bolt joints
Millimetres. Import STLs at 100% scale.

Reprint the R4 nozzle and R4 front cover together. Their cover mounting tabs
have changed. Keep the R2 71 mm propeller and 22.6 mm motor insert; both are
included for convenience. Do not print the reference motor or metal fasteners.

COVER HARDWARE (four identical joints)
4 x M3 x 12 mm socket cap screws, length measured below the head
8 x M3 flat washers, nominal OD 7 / ID 3.2 / thickness 0.5 mm
4 x M3 hex nuts, nominal AF 5.5 / thickness 2.4 mm
2.5 mm Allen key

The holes are 3.4 mm clearance holes, not printed screw threads. Nut pockets
are 5.9 mm across flats. Cover tabs are rounded, with flat washer/clamping
lands. Each screw clamps 5 mm cover tab + 3 mm nozzle seat + two washers
and a full 2.4 mm nut. Nominal thread projection is 0.6 mm beyond the nut.

ASSEMBLY ORDER
1. Remove support material and burrs from holes, flat lands and T-slots.
2. Before fitting the cover, slide one rear washer into each upper side slot
   from inside the nozzle, then slide a nut into the hex pocket below it.
3. Seat the cover tabs in the four nozzle sockets. Fit a front washer and
   M3x12 screw at each joint. Start all four screws loosely before tightening
   in opposite pairs. Tighten by hand only; do not crush the PLA tabs.
4. Disconnect power and turn the propeller through a full revolution. Check
   the complete circumference, motor alignment, and shaft end clearance.
   Resolve any contact before applying power.

OTHER HARDWARE RETAINED
4 x M3x8 propeller screws (no washers in the modeled recesses)
4 x M3x8 motor-base screws + 4 x M3 flat washers
4 x M4x12 frame bolts + 4 x M4 flat washers + 4 x M4 nuts

The motor coupling, 22.6 mm insert, 71 mm blade sweep, motor-centering cup
and continuous rear wire ring retain their R3 geometry and placement.
The cover's dome and central shaft bore are unchanged. Only its outer
mounting tabs and their local washer/tool access have been revised.

PRINT / FIT
Use the Bambu P1S PLA Basic profile, 0.16 mm layers and six walls as prototype
starting settings. Support the nozzle tab pocket roofs as needed;
remove supports completely from nut and washer insertion slots. Inspect the
slicer preview before printing. Keep supports off the propeller drive seats
where possible. Deburr the blade tips and duct wall without changing the hub.
Printed holes and real motor dimensions can vary. CAD clearance and mesh
checks do not guarantee physical fit or powered performance. Confirm nut,
washer and motor fit and free rotation on the actual parts before use.

Validation evidence and the native assembly STEP are included in this package.
