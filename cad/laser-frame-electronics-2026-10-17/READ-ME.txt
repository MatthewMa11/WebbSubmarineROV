ROV LASER-CUT FRAME AND ELECTRONICS CYLINDER
17 October 2026

WHAT WAS ADDED
Eight flat 3 mm aluminum frame pieces attach the existing eight thrusters:
two decks, two electronics-tube bulkheads, and four vertical-thruster supports.
The upper deck has large access windows; both decks have four 96 mm openings
on the vertical thruster axes. Tab joints use modeled M3 bolts, washers and
captured nuts. All four horizontal thrusters mount to the lower deck. The
vertical supports carry the other four thrusters and join both decks.

The central horizontal electronics tube has 160 mm OD, 148 mm ID, 6 mm wall
and 200 mm body length. Its axis is X and its centre is (0,0,55) mm. Two 8 mm
end plates make a 216 mm cap-to-cap length. End plates are 196 mm diameter;
four external M4 rods secure them through the two frame bulkheads. The hollow
body has about 3.44 litres before electronics are installed. No electronics
are assumed, and internal equipment mounts can be designed around the actual
boards, battery and wiring later.

The tube and end plates are an enclosure layout. O-rings, sealing features,
cable glands and a working-depth pressure rating have not been selected or
validated. Do not treat this CAD cylinder as a qualified waterproof housing.
Frame strength, material grade and physical fits also require verification.

LASER-CUT FILES
Use laser-cut-DXF/ at 100 percent scale, in millimetres. Cut one of each LC01
through LC08 from 3 mm aluminum. DXFs contain CUT geometry only; labels in the
profile preview are not engraving instructions. Profiles have no kerf offset.
Use the cutter's compensation and verify a stock/slot/nut fit coupon first.
Slots are nominally 3.2 mm for 3 mm sheet. The two decks each need at least
a 520 x 500 mm usable cutting area; profiles have not been nested on stock.
LC03 and LC04 are the two bulkheads; LC05-LC08 are the four upright supports.

End plates, cylindrical tube and purchased fasteners are separate from these
3 mm laser-cut parts. The end plates are reference solid disks with four
4.3 mm rod holes on a 178 mm pitch circle, at 45,135,225 and 315 degrees.
Any final sealing faces/grooves need a separately completed enclosure design.

THRUSTER MOUNTING
The motor, nozzle, propeller and all eight shaft positions/directions are kept.
Each thruster's four M4 mounting bolts and four washers move outward by 3 mm
to make room for the sheet between washer and nozzle pad. Original M4 nuts
stay in place. The existing M4x12 shafts still pass through the full nut;
check the actual printed seats and hardware during assembly. Access holes
clear the nearby cover-retention screw heads. Other source parts stay put.

The R3 source already had imported cover and propeller geometry faults. Their
surfaces are preserved here; this task does not repair those inherited faults.

ADDITIONAL HARDWARE
24 M3x16 socket-head bolts, 24 M3 washers (7 OD x 3.2 ID x 0.5 mm), and
24 M3 hex nuts (5.5 AF x 2.4 mm) fasten the sheet tab joints.
4 M4 threaded tie rods, nominal model length 230 mm; 8 M4 washers
(9 OD x 4.3 ID x 0.8 mm) and 8 M4 hex nuts (7 AF x 3.2 mm) retain the pod.
These are extra to the existing eight complete thruster assemblies.

VERIFICATION
All eight new sheet pieces and 103 added components are valid single solids.
The new parts have no unintended solid intersections. All eight thrusters
clear the added frame, cylinder and new hardware. Exported DXFs have closed
paths, and all four vertical axes retain an unobstructed 76 mm diameter
passage through the new frame. Native STEP read-back confirms 415 solids.
The complete frame envelope is approximately 520 x 500 x 225 mm including
fastener heads. Details are recorded in frame-pod-verification.json.
