EIGHT-THRUSTER ROV LAYOUT - 17 October 2026

Four complete A2212 motor/propeller/nozzle subassemblies point outward at
45 degrees to the horizontal X/Y axes. Four further subassemblies have
vertical shafts, arranged as two up and two down on opposite diagonals.

Source: the saved 39-body R3 centering-cup assembly. All original parts,
motor references and fasteners are retained. This is an independent layout
snapshot; later edits to the single-thruster design do not update this file.

Open ROV8-native.step for the assembly with the original Onshape STEP
geometry entities preserved. The root contains eight thruster occurrences,
each with 39 parts: 312 solid instances total. Coordinates and size use mm.
The native source STEP uses metres internally; importing normally converts
it to the document's display units without scaling.

Horizontal shaft origins: (+/-190, +/-75, 0) mm.
Vertical shaft origins: (+/-75, +/-170, 0) mm.
Overall envelope: approximately 488 x 446 x 108 mm.
All 28 pairs of conservative boxes around the complete thrusters have
positive separation; the smallest box-to-box gap is 18.7867 mm. This is
a conservative thruster-to-thruster clearance, not a frame-mount check.

No vehicle frame or connection brackets have been added. The mounting pads
and original fasteners remain part of each thruster. Motor pointing direction
does not establish actual thrust direction or ESC reversibility.

assembly-verification.json records placements, axes, STEP read-back and
pairwise spacing. thruster-positions.csv lists all eight placements. Strict
OCCT B-rep checks flag pre-existing source surfaces; this placement task
preserves those source entities and does not claim to repair them.
The visualization is a CAD preview, not a physical prototype test.
