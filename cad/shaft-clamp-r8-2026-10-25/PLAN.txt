DESIGN
Two-piece external shaft clamp: the fan carries the lower jaw; a separate
rounded upper strap closes around the exposed shaft with two opposed M3x16
socket screws and two M3 nuts. Bolt/nut orientations oppose each other for
hardware symmetry. The four existing solid drive keys continue carrying torque;
the shaft clamp provides axial retention. Nothing enters the former insert
region. Main frame, cap and motor geometry and poses are preserved.
