R6.1 — flatter rear motor boss — 24 October 2026

本次只压低电机后方带四个检修孔的凸起：
圆顶约削低 12 mm，端面改平，外沿 R1 mm 圆角，四个孔口 R0.3 mm 圆角。
四个 7.6 mm 检修孔、电机安装座、承力底板和定位杯保持原位。
风扇、R6 电机夹紧结构、另一侧前盖及全部 42 个紧固件不变。

The rear dome is approximately 12 mm shorter. Its new flat end is at Y=-30 mm.
The solid material behind the central blind pocket retains 7 mm of thickness.
This edit removes local rear-tip material only; it does not move the motor.

STL files: MILLIMETRES, 100% scale. Print one of each if making a complete set.
Nozzle-R6p1-flat-motor-boss.stl — NEW nozzle/frame with the flatter rear boss.
Propeller-R6-integral-M3-clamp.stl — unchanged R6 one-piece propeller and clamp.
Front-cap-R4-unchanged.stl — unchanged opposite/front cap.
If you already printed the R6 propeller and front cap, reuse them; print only the
new nozzle. Replace the previous nozzle STL with the R6.1 file in this package.

Motor attachment remains the R6 design:
two M3x16 socket-head bolts, two captured M3 nuts and two M3 flat washers.
Four solid keys engage the reference motor's four openings on an 18 mm pitch
circle. There is no separate printed spacer inside the motor bell.

Assembly remains the same. Mount the stationary motor base using its four M3x8
screws and washers. Tighten evenly. Keep the front cap off while aligning the
four propeller drive keys and installing the two M3 clamp bolt/nut/washer stacks.
Tighten the opposed clamp bolts gradually and alternately; do not force the
closure stops shut. Fit the front cap last with its retained through-bolts.

Use the previous calibrated P1S/PLA print setup, millimetres and 100% scale.
Inspect supports in the motor seat, access holes and clamping surfaces before
assembly. Keep the prior nozzle print orientation; the new central flat face
is not necessarily the lowest face of the whole nozzle.

After assembly, with power disconnected, rotate several complete turns and
check for rubbing, tilt, looseness or pull-off. The geometry/mesh report and
native interference check validate the CAD revision, not physical printed
strength, motor variation or a rated operating speed.
