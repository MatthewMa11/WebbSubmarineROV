What changed
The gold cone's inner flat face now has a smooth spherical recess, nominally
24mm wide and8mm deep with a0.6mm rounded rim. The obsolete blind3.5mm passage
is filled, leaving a continuous bowl. Exterior cone profile remains unchanged.
Conservative metal-adapter rotation clearance increases from1.77 to6.09mm.
The fan and actual motor, collet, collar and bullet nut retain their R11/R12
geometry and positions. No internal printed motor spacer is added.

Cone attachment now uses four M3x12 socket screws and real captured M3 nuts,
in place of the previous M3x40 screws. Four0.8mm locating pilots fit6.8mm cone
recesses around6.4mm pilots, with0.2mm radial/axial relief. This aligns the cap
before tightening. Local3.6mm bolt-clearance holes ease printed assembly.

Use the MATCHED R13 housing and cone. Reprint BOTH; their new mating and nut
features differ from R12. Reuse the unchanged fan and metal hardware except
for replacing the four long cap screws with M3x12 screws from your assortment.

Joint fit check and cap installation
1. Use one M3x12 socket screw, one M3 hex nut and TWO M3 FLAT washers, one under
   the screw head and one behind the housing bearing roof. The CAD washer is
   7mm OD x3.2mm ID x0.5mm thick; do not substitute a split spring washer here.
2. Hold a nut with a flat washer on it, and slide the stack inward from the
   OUTSIDE opening of the housing post. The wide upper slot carries the washer;
   the narrower lower slot captures the nut's flats. The slot allows the loose
   stack to sit about0.25mm below its final clamped position while loading.
   Its surfaces must be free of support residue. The old deep rear pockets
   are unused; do not put the cap nuts there.
3. Place the cone's underside recesses over the housing pilots. Its four flat
   feet must sit against the housing seats. The pilots locate the cap but must
   not hold it up; nominal pilot-end relief is0.2mm. No forcing is needed.
4. Put a front flat washer beneath each M3x12 screw head, insert from the cone
   side and start it in the captured nut. The nut should remain held by its
   pocket, without a wrench. Tighten the four screws gradually in a cross
   pattern, only enough for firm seating. Do not crush the PLA.
5. Check the joint coupons first: screw passes freely, nut/washer stack slides
   in, pilots seat, nut cannot spin, and faces clamp without a gap. Expected
   full nut engagement is2.4mm, with0.9mm tip protrusion (about two threads).
   If your hardware or print differs, correct the fit before the full print.

Motor/fan assembly remains R11/R12
With cone removed and power disconnected, install the bench-assembled actual
motor/collet/collar/fan/bullet nut through the front opening. The R11 fan uses
5.1mm stud clearance and6.2mm shallow rear relief. Route wires through the
existing rear ports and out of rotating parts. Keep FOUR M3x8 motor-base bolts
with TWO0.5mm flat washers under EACH head; these base bolts are unchanged.
External M4 mounting hardware also stays unchanged. Fit the cone last using
the new front-loading joint procedure above.
