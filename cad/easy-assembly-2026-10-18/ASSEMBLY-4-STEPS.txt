EASY-ASSEMBLY ROV - 18 October 2026

1. BUILD THE FIVE-PANEL FRAME
   Cut A once, B twice, and C twice from 3 mm aluminum. Insert the tabs of
   the two identical side plates and two identical open saddles into base A.
   Put one M4 nut into each of the eight accessible cross pockets. Fit the
   eight M4x16 bolts with washers from underneath. Start all eight loosely,
   square the panels to the base, then tighten evenly. No upper deck is fitted.

2. FIT THE EIGHT PREASSEMBLED THRUSTERS
   Bolt the four horizontal units to the four diagonal mounting patterns on
   the base, pointing outward toward the corners. Bolt the four vertical
   units to the two short side plates. Follow labels H1-H4 and V1 UP,
   V2 DOWN, V3 UP, V4 DOWN in the CAD assembly. Retain the existing M4
   thruster mounting hardware; all thruster internal parts are unchanged.

3. PLACE THE ELECTRONICS POD
   Thread two 25 mm release straps through the paired base slots beside the
   saddles. Lower the complete cylinder, caps and tie rods into the open
   saddles. The two small open notches in each saddle clear the lower rods.
   Use a suitable thin liner between the saddle and real tube; the CAD has
   0.5 mm nominal radial saddle clearance. The pod is not threaded through rings.

4. CLOSE TWO BUCKLES
   Close and adjust the two straps from above. To remove the pod, disconnect
   its leads and release the two straps, then lift it straight out. The CAD
   checks a continuous 200 mm lift with the straps released. The pod's end-cap
   nuts need not be removed to take the complete pod out of the frame.

The strap and buckle shapes are purchased-part layout references. Select
actual wet-service straps and retention capacity for the real loads. Physical
strength, joint fit, sealing and cable glands remain to be validated.
