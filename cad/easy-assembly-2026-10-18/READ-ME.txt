SIMPLIFIED ROV - FIVE FRAME PANELS, EIGHT FRAME JOINTS
18 October 2026

WHAT CHANGED
Frame panels: 8 -> 5. Unique cutting shapes: 3. Frame joint bolts: 24 -> 8.
The upper deck and tall uprights are removed. Two identical short side plates
carry the four vertical thrusters. Two identical open saddles replace the
closed tube bulkheads. Two adjustable straps allow the pod to lift out.
The added frame/pod/retention assembly drops from 103 to 56 solid references.
The complete vehicle STEP contains 368 solid instances, including the original
312 thruster bodies. These counts include fastener reference geometry.

The eight thruster positions and directions stay unchanged. The 3 mm aluminum
sheet, 160 OD x 148 ID x 200 mm body-length electronics tube, end caps and
pod tie rods are retained. There is approximately 3.44 litres inside the tube.
The frame footprint remains 520 x 500 mm. Removing the upper deck gives direct
access to the cylinder and thrusters. The frame joints use M4x16 bolts, M4
washers and M4 hex nuts, with accessible nut pockets and underside bolt heads.
Thruster-internal M3 hardware is unchanged.

CUTTING FILES - laser-cut-unique/
A-base-QTY1.dxf: cut ONE, nominal flat envelope 520 x 500 mm.
B-side-plate-QTY2.dxf: cut TWO identical pieces, about 240 x 84 mm.
C-open-saddle-QTY2.dxf: cut TWO identical pieces, about 190 x 79 mm.
All use 3 mm aluminum, millimetres and 100 percent scale. Paths are nominal,
without kerf compensation or text engraving. Set the laser's compensation and
check a stock/slot/nut fit coupon before cutting the full set. The base needs
a usable cutting area of at least 520 x 500 mm. No stock nesting is supplied.
The duplicate files are intentionally consolidated into three unique profiles.

Read ASSEMBLY-4-STEPS.txt for the assembly order. Start joints loosely before
final tightening. The strap/buckle envelopes are references for purchased
adjustable 25 mm straps, not production drawings for a buckle mechanism.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
All five panels and 56 added components are valid single solids. New parts do
not have unintended solid intersections and clear all eight thrusters. DXF
lines/arcs/circles form closed CUT paths in mm. Four vertical thruster paths
retain a 76 mm clear passage. Conservative continuous envelopes check a
200 mm upward lift of the released pod, including its caps and tie rods.

This is an assembly simplification, not a structural or pressure qualification.
Removing the upper deck changes stiffness; real loads and joint retention
need physical validation. Tube seals, cable glands and a working-depth rating
are not designed or validated. The R3 source cover/propeller already carried
imported geometry faults; those surfaces are preserved and not repaired here.
