CAD qualification scope - 15 October 2026

The approved revision preserves the gray cover, propeller, insert and motor
reference exactly. Only localized nozzle screw access/retention features and
17 added hardware solids changed. Both the actual native exported positions
and independent conservative hardware envelopes were checked.

39 solids yield 741 possible pairs. The only positive overlaps are the four
modeled M3 major-diameter shafts in the printed insert's 2.6 mm thread pilots.
Helical threads and their strength are not modeled. Native Onshape interference
detection independently reports the same four pairs and no others.

Continuous coaxial enclosing volumes contain the seven moving bodies and are
clear of all stationary bodies. This covers angles between animation frames.
The front portion uses a tighter axial envelope so clearance of the cover
retention nuts and pins is established without assuming the whole 19 mm-deep
propeller is a solid disk at every radius. A native 360-degree animation passed.

The inherited original nozzle/cover toroidal end-cap surfaces may trigger
boundary warnings when STEP is imported into OCCT. Native geometry was not
changed to satisfy that second kernel. Native Onshape regeneration and every
delivered STL's independent closed-edge/orientation tests pass.

Fit pieces A/B/C are full-scale crops of the native printable meshes. D/E/F are
explicit nominal gauges copying the relevant bore, nut-pocket and motor-base
seat dimensions; they omit the curved outer shell and are not replacement parts.
They are not an FEA test, a balancing test or a guarantee of printer accuracy.

Physical fit, winding clearance, actual motor variants, shaft runout, print
strength, fastener loosening, water performance and durability remain untested.
See physical-fit-record.csv: every physical check is correctly marked NOT TESTED.
