WHAT CHANGED
Only the nozzle's fastener access and retention seats changed. The gray cover,
propeller, motor insert and motor reference retain their geometry and placement.
Four motor-base access pockets are now diameter 7.6 with rounded entrances and
flat washer seats. The kit's socket heads and M3 flat washers fit these pockets.
Three cover bosses now have recessed washer seats and AF5.8 captive-nut pockets.
The actual cover has three 4 mm blind holes, not through-holes. M3x8 bolts locked
by nuts captured in the nozzle act as retention pins in these existing holes.
Their tips stop approximately 0.996 short of the blind-hole floors. Do not
substitute longer bolts or force threads into the unchanged cover.

The native assembly contains 39 solids: six mechanical bodies and 33 hardware
parts. The original eight screws are retained; seven further screws complete
the modeled motor-base and cover retention. All screw sizes are in your kit.

PRINT THE FIT CHECKS FIRST
A: Actual motor hub mating surfaces with short blade-root stubs. Check the
   actual bell seat, all four drive keys, screw-head access and 18 mm pattern.
B: Actual motor insert, including the four M3 pilots. Check it sits level in
   the real rotor without force. Prepare the pilots for M3x0.5 threads gently.
C: Actual mounting-pad crop. Check four CORNER M4 bolts, washers and nuts.
   The other holes are alternative positions; eight nuts do not fit at once.
D: A nominal 3.5 mm x 15 mm shaft-bore gauge. The real 3.175 mm shaft must pass
   and turn freely. It represents the unchanged cover bore, not a new cover.
E: A nominal replica of the captive nut, washer and bolt seat, including the
   actual 0.15 rounds. Check the M3 nut slides in and seats without cracking it.
F: A nominal motor-base seating gauge with the actual 19 x 16 mm hole pattern,
   5 mm mounting thickness, 7.6 pockets and 3.3 bores. The curved access tunnel
   is omitted. M3x8 plus the 0.5 washer projects 2.5 beyond the mounting face.
A/B/C retain native mating geometry; D/E/F are dimensionally matched gauges.

P1S / PLA BASIC STARTING SETUP
Assumes the stock 0.4 nozzle; select your actual installed nozzle in Bambu Studio.
Use the PLA Basic filament preset and the correct preset for your actual plate.
Keep the preset temperatures and cooling as the starting point.
Use 0.16 layers, six walls and six top/bottom layers. Start outer walls around
60 mm/s for the fit-sensitive prototype. These are engineering starting settings,
not a physically qualified print process or a guarantee of strength.
Use 100 percent infill for the propeller and insert; start with 50 percent gyroid
for the nozzle and cover. Use matching settings on their corresponding gauges.
Do not globally scale the model or blindly add hole compensation. Measure a
failed fit first; changing every hole can weaken the insert's threaded pilots.
The complete STL files retain the assembly axes. Orient them before slicing:
- Nozzle: its flat M4 mounting pad is a possible build face. Preview supports
  throughout the duct, motor cradle and wire collar before committing the print.
- Propeller: front screw-seat face on the plate, motor axis vertical; support
  blade overhangs as needed while keeping mating seats and holes clean.
- Insert: flat ring face down, bore axes vertical.
- Cover: rear feet down with motor axis vertical; support floating underside
  regions as needed. Keep the central shaft bore clear of support residue.
Inspect the slicer preview for unsupported islands and incomplete thin walls.
Main-part support cleanup and horizontal holes still need checking even when
a vertically printed gauge fits. Gauges cannot prove every print orientation.

ASSEMBLE AND CHECK WITHOUT POWER
1. Seat the three M3 cover nuts in the nozzle before installing the cover.
2. Attach the stator base using four M3x8 screws and four 0.5 flat washers.
   The reference has 3 mm-deep M3 holes: modeled engagement is 2.5 with 0.5 tip
   margin. Verify your motor's real threads and depth before tightening.
3. Install the inner adapter level. Use four M3x8 propeller screws without
   washers. Modeled pilot engagement is 3, with 1.4 tip margin. Tighten evenly
   and gently in PLA; no pull-out strength or tightening torque was validated.
4. Hand-turn the rotor before fitting the cover. There must be no winding rub;
   the reference CAD omits the winding volume.
5. Fit the unchanged cover and its three M3x8 retention bolts with flat washers.
   Nuts lock the bolts in the nozzle. The pins retain the cover; they are not
   through-bolts clamping a nut behind its blind-hole floors. Check cover seating,
   centering and movement while hand-turning through several full revolutions.
6. Install the four corner M4x12 frame bolts, 0.8 washers and ordinary M4 nuts.
   The actual vehicle bracket is not modeled. Inward insertion must be 8.0 to
   13.5: bolt length minus external bracket/washer stack. The modeled M4x12 and
   0.8 washer permit up to 3.2 additional bracket thickness.

GO / NO-GO BEFORE POWER
The propeller must turn without touching the nozzle, cover, wires or fasteners.
The motor seat and insert must fit without forcing the bell or rubbing windings.
The cover must remain centered and secure, and the shaft must turn freely after
the cover is installed. Its nominal radial clearance is only 0.1625 per side.
If any check fails, stop and record the measured fit in physical-fit-record.csv.
Do not force it or assume CAD alignment removes print error or motor runout.
After the hand-spin checks pass, use a restrained, guarded water test at low
throttle, within the actual motor/ESC ratings. Check vibration, current, rubbing
and fastener loosening before increasing load. Stop on any abnormal result.

WHAT THE VERIFICATION ESTABLISHES
35 current CAD checks pass: 12 hardware/rotation checks, 19 mounting checks,
and four wire/head-clearance checks. All 39 native solid positions were checked,
including all 17 newly added hardware bodies. Native Onshape interference
analysis reports only the four intentional M3-to-insert pilot contacts. The
native revolute animation completed 360 degrees with the hardware present.
All ten delivered STL files are single closed, consistently oriented meshes,
with no bad edges or degenerate triangles.
Conservative propeller clearance remains at least 0.55 to the nozzle, about 1
to the cover and 2 above the screw heads. The enclosed wire route remains 10 mm
with a nominal 4 mm wall. Motor guide radial fit is 0.20; insert fit is 0.25.
The drive keys have assembly clearance and are not a zero-backlash coupling.

Operational reliability, printed strength, balance, thrust and durability are
not established by CAD alone. Your motor is unavailable for fit testing until
after printing, so no honest operational guarantee can be issued at this stage.
