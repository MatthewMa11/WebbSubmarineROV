WHAT CHANGED AFTER YOUR PRINT
The previous design left only about 0.55 mm conservative blade-tip clearance.
Your photos show rough inner duct material and your video shows the rubbing
problem. The photos cannot establish whether the motor is also tilted or the
hub is seated unevenly. The earlier CAD checks did not establish printed fit.

The new propeller's complete swept diameter is 71 mm. Only the blade tips were
trimmed and their new edges rounded. The hub, motor guide, drive keys, screw
centers and screw seats are unchanged. Nominal conservative tip clearance to
the existing duct increases to approximately 2.45 mm per side.

The spacer's outside envelope reduces from 23.2 to 22.6 mm, with rounded 0.2 mm
outer lead-ins. Its thickness, 18 mm screw pitch circle and four M3 pilots stay
unchanged. This removes 0.3 mm radially at the outer tips. Actual motor inside
diameter was not supplied, so print this small part first and check its fit.

The single side wire collar is removed. The nozzle now has a centered, rounded
rear guide ring: 66 mm outside diameter, 56 mm inside diameter, 6 mm thickness,
0.8 mm lip rounds, and four 5 mm support posts outside the opening. The central
pod is limited to 31 mm diameter at this exit. This leaves a 12.5 mm-wide annular
wire window all around it. Route wires between the fixed forward ribs and out
through any rear sector; keep them clear of the rotating bell and blades.
The guide is not a substitute for securing the cable against movement.

PRINT AND ASSEMBLE
Use your P1S, actual nozzle/plate preset and PLA Basic preset. With a 0.4 mm
nozzle, 0.16 mm layers, six walls, six top/bottom layers and solid propeller/
spacer infill are starting settings. Start the nozzle at 50% gyroid infill.
These are prototype settings, not tested strength values.
The native STL files retain the assembly axes; orient parts before slicing.
Preview the rear guide ring, its posts and the inner duct for supports and
unsupported islands. Clean support residue, strings and blobs from the duct
and seating faces. Avoid supports damaging the motor guide and screw seats.

Before attaching the propeller, confirm the spacer slips fully into the real
bell and sits level without being forced. Do not hammer or pull it into place
with screws. Prepare its M3 pilots gently; excessive screw force can split PLA.
Seat the motor base flat and tighten all four base screws evenly in a diagonal
sequence, using the original M3x8 and 0.5 mm washers. Check real thread depth.
The propeller must sit level on the motor guide with all four drive keys seated.
Tighten its four M3x8 screws evenly without washers, avoiding distortion.

HAND-SPIN BEFORE POWER
Turn several full revolutions with the motor fixed, first without and then
with the original gray cover installed. There must be no contact, visible
wobble, wire drag, or cover movement. Inspect the tip gap at several angles.
The gray cover's shaft bore is unchanged and remains a close fit; make sure
the real shaft turns freely. Correct uneven seating before applying power.
After that passes, perform a restrained, guarded low-power water test, checking
for rubbing, imbalance, excessive current and loosening before increasing load.

VERIFICATION SCOPE
Fresh native STEP and STL exports were used for the R2 checks. Checks cover the
continuous blade envelope, stated blade-to-duct error allowance, unbroken ring
opening, closed meshes, unchanged hub/fastener geometry and cover preservation.
The reference stator is a base-only model; real windings are not modeled.
The native 39-part assembly completed a full 360-degree mate animation. Native
interference analysis reports only the four intended M3-to-printed-pilot contacts.

The blade allowance calculation assumes 0.5 mm eccentricity, 0.3 mm inward duct
print error, 0.2 mm blade growth and 1 degree tilt over 30 mm. These are explicit
design allowances, not measurements of your print. They do not prove the close
shaft bore, motor winding clearance, dynamic balance, strength or thrust.
The new parts have not yet been physically tested. Do the hand-spin check above.
