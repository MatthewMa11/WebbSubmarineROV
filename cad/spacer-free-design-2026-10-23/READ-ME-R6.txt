ASSEMBLY
1. Remove support residue and burrs, especially from the bell bore, four drive keys,
   bolt holes and hex nut pockets. Do not enlarge a hole by forcing hardware into it.
2. Mount the stationary motor base to the existing frame. Tighten its four screws
   evenly so the motor axis stays centered. Confirm the motor bell turns freely.
3. Keep the cap off. Preload one M3 nut in each of the two opposed clamp pockets.
4. Align the four solid propeller keys with the four motor-bell openings and slide
   the propeller onto the OUTSIDE of the bell. The keys carry torque; the clamp
   retains the propeller axially. It must seat without force or a tilted hub.
5. Fit an M3 washer under each M3x16 bolt head, insert both bolts, then tighten
   alternately in small increments. Use only enough clamping to prevent slip.
   The 0.6 mm stop gaps limit ear travel; they are not a target to force shut.
   Stop if the bell distorts, the motor drags, PLA cracks, or the clamp bottoms
   before gripping. No tightening torque has been established by physical testing.
6. Fit the unchanged cap last using its existing through-bolt hardware.
7. With power disconnected, rotate through several complete turns. Check every
   blade tip, the cap and the supports. Check that the propeller cannot pull off
   or rotate independently of the bell. Remove wire slack from the rotating area.
8. A supervised low-speed physical trial and balance/retention checks are still
   required before increasing speed. CAD checks do not certify printed strength,
   motor variation, thrust performance or successful powered operation.

P1S / PLA BASIC STARTING PRINT SETTINGS
Use your calibrated PLA profile, 0.16 mm layers, 5 walls and about 40% infill.
Place the propeller's flat front hub face on the bed; its motor-facing opening
points upward. Use supports under blade/ear overhangs as the slicer preview needs.
Keep support contacts out of the bell bore and off the four drive keys where
possible. Remove all supports; inspect each blade root and clamp slit for damage.
Use the existing orientations/settings for the unchanged frame and cap.
These are starting settings, not validated production settings or a strength rating.
